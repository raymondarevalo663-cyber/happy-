To my lovely misis, Maria Veronica 💗

Happy Monthsary po, misis kooo hehe 💗 I just want to thank you po for all the love, patience, and for staying with me through our laughs, talks, tampuhan, misunderstandings, and even the hard days

I know our story is not always easy, especially because of the distance, but I want you to know that I am still here and I will keep choosing you po 💗 I love your personality, your laugh, your determination, your cuteness, and all the little things that make you ikaw

One of the things that makes me really happy is when we talk about our future and the family we dream of having together 🥹 When we talk about our baby boy, our little Utoy, and how we want to name him Aeron Von Rayldred M Arevalo, I catch myself smiling because I can imagine us being parents together someday

I also think about Via, our little one who is already one year old 💗 I love hearing you talk about her and imagining how we will care for her, guide her, and help her grow up feeling safe, loved, and understood

I imagine us teaching our children to be kind, respectful, honest, and God-fearing, and to know that they can always talk to us when something is bothering them 🥹 I want us to listen to them, guide them without making them scared to tell us the truth, support their dreams, and teach them right from wrong with love and patience

I pray that someday we will look back at these talks and smile because we made it through the distance and built the home we used to imagine together 🙏💗 Mahal na mahal kita, misis ko, from July 18 to all the tomorrows God gives us

Always yours,
Your Mister, RayRay
